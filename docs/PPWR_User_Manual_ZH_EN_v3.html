推荐使用顺序 / Recommended sequence

建项目并核对适用性 → 建立包装/BOM → 阅读逐项指引并采集证据 → 预检及提交 → 专家审核 → 生成并签发文件 → 跟踪供应商和法规变化。

Create a project and verify applicability → register packaging/BOM → follow item guidance and collect evidence → pre-check and submit → expert review → generate and issue documents → monitor supplier and regulatory changes.

新增重点：专家人工更新知识库不是普通文件上传。只有经专家检查并发布后，新条目/规则才进入知识检索与 AI 初审。
New focus: an expert manual knowledge update is not a routine file upload. A new entry/rule joins retrieval and AI pre-review only after expert review and publication.

PPWR Digital Platform02 · Project

创建项目与文件场景
Create Project & Document Scenario

Dashboard → New TD / DoC project
  1. 填写项目/产品标题、PS 编号、目标日期和用途；专家选择客户。按真实用途设定食品接触、可重复使用、Art 9 类型、生物基声明。Enter project/product title, PS ID, target date and use; experts select the client. Set food-contact, reusable, Article 9 type and biobased flags according to facts.
  2. 场景 A：每个包装单元独立 TD/DoC，另有文件包总览；场景 B：一个产品的全部单元合并为一套 TD/DoC。需按制造商和文件责任范围选择。Scenario A: TD/DoC per packaging unit plus package overview. Scenario B: one consolidated TD/DoC for all units of a product. Choose according to manufacturer/document scope.
  3. 可同时创建第一个包装单元；点击 Create project。之后 Overview 可编辑项目属性；修改后复核适用性、已关闭条目及文件状态。Optionally create the first packaging unit and select Create project. Edit profile later in Overview; recheck applicability, closed items and document status after changes.

项目总览 / Project overview

检查未收集、待初审、待专家、退回返工和关闭数量。项目级条目和每个包装单元的条目都影响进度；使用 Compliance guide 查看按法规级别排序的缺口。

Monitor uncollected, awaiting AI, awaiting expert, rejected and closed items. Project-level and unit-level items both affect progress; use Compliance guide for gaps by legal level.

平台用于组织证据、审核和生成文档，不代表监管批准或制造商签署。
The platform organizes evidence/review and generates documents; it does not constitute regulatory approval or manufacturer signature.

PPWR Digital Platform03 · Compliance Guide

合规指引与法律级别
Compliance Guide & Legal Levels

Project → Compliance guide
  1. 选包装单元，查看实施时间线、法规动态、开放证据、截止日期、最低证据和缺口；用级别筛选并点条目进入填写。Select a unit. Review timeline, regulatory alerts, open evidence, due dates, minimum evidence and gaps; filter by level and open an item.
  2. 区分 Legally required now、Required from a later date、Recommended evidence 和 Good practice。推荐/良好实践不应表述为法定要求。Distinguish current legal requirements, future requirements, recommended evidence and good practice. Do not describe recommendations/practices as legal duties.
  3. 检查条款依据和适用日期；“最低证据”进度是平台配置的证据检查，不等于专家接受。发现适用性错误，应修正项目画像/BOM并重新查看。Check provision and application date. Minimum-evidence progress reflects configured checks, not expert acceptance. Correct profile/BOM and recheck if applicability is wrong.
标签 / Label解释 / Meaning
Legally required now对本包装适用且现已生效；按期限和最低证据处理。
Applies to this packaging and is currently in force; follow deadline/minimum evidence.
Required from a later date未来生效，可提前准备。
Future requirement; may be prepared in advance.
Recommended / Good practice支持证明或减少问询，但不是法定必交文件。
May support evidence or reduce questions; not automatically a legally mandated document.
“Required”结论须结合适用性、日期与来源。遇到法规解释疑问，交专家核对正式文本，不以 AI 摘要代替法规判断。
Any “required” conclusion depends on applicability, date and source. Refer interpretation questions to an expert and official text, not an AI summary.

PPWR Digital Platform04 · Packaging & BOM

包装单元与 BOM
Packaging Units & BOM

Project → Packaging & BOM
  1. 新增包装单元，录入名称、层级、类型、UPI、SKU、质量、尺寸、供应商/地点。Add a unit with name, level, type, UPI, SKU, mass, dimensions and supplier/site.
  2. 选择单元并新增 BOM 组件：将纸、塑料、盖、标签、油墨和黏合剂分行，填写结构、材料组、质量和再生含量。Select the unit and add BOM components. Record paper, plastic, cap, label, ink and adhesive separately; enter structure, material group, mass and recycled content.
  3. 将组件链接至供应商材料库，核对组件质量合计、材料分类、SKU 和来源。C01 包装属性请回到登记页修改。Link components to supplier materials; verify total mass, classification, SKU and source. Edit C01 packaging properties in the register.

传统 BOM 工作簿 / Traditional BOM workbook

下载 PPWR_BOM_V1.xlsx，保留表头、一行一组件；在 Packaging & BOM 导入。导入按编号创建/更新。应先导出备份并确认项目，再核查导入结果。

Download PPWR_BOM_V1.xlsx, keep headers and use one row per component; import from Packaging & BOM. Import creates/updates by ID. Export a copy and verify project/IDs first; inspect results after import.

修改已批准属性可能重开相关条目；删除单元会删除其组件和采集项。核对并导出后再执行删除。
Changing approved properties may reopen related items; deleting a unit removes components and collection items. Review and export before deletion.

PPWR Digital Platform05 · Smart Import

智能文件与表格导入
Smart File & Table Import

Project → Smart import
  1. 拖入文件/文件夹/ZIP，或选 Choose files/Choose folder。支持 PDF、DOCX、XLSX、CSV、图片、ZIP；遵守页面显示总量上限。以附录 A 目录名和有条目编号的文件名提高匹配质量。Drop files/folder/ZIP or use Choose files/Choose folder. PDF, DOCX, XLSX, CSV, images and ZIP are supported; observe displayed total limit. Appendix A folders and item-coded filenames improve matching.
  2. 在暂存结果核对置信度、条目、单元、类型、匹配依据及摘录；纠正错误或选择 skip。低置信度/未识别项需人工指定，不要猜测。Review confidence, item, unit, type, matching rationale and excerpt. Correct or skip; manually classify low-confidence/unmatched files rather than guessing.
  3. 选择默认单元，确认是否自动提交和运行 AI 初审，再点 One-click import。可先保存更正或丢弃暂存批次。Select a default unit and decide whether to auto-submit/pre-review; then use One-click import. Corrections can be saved first or the staged batch discarded.
  4. 表格数据可粘贴或上传 XLSX/CSV；点 Parse table，核对每列映射和预期行数，再导入。单位列可创建/更新包装单元，BOM 列新增组件，条目列填入字段。Paste/upload XLSX/CSV, parse, verify column mappings and row count, then import. Unit columns create/update units, BOM columns add components, item columns populate fields.
扫描 PDF/图片在当前试点无 OCR，可能仅按文件名匹配。导入前后均要对照原文件；导入/自动提交会写入真实数据，已批准条目不会被表格覆盖。
The pilot has no OCR for scanned PDFs/images; matching may use filenames only. Compare against originals before/after import. Import/auto-submit writes real data; approved items are not overwritten by table import.

查看 Import history 只能确认批次操作记录，不代表审核通过。

Import history confirms batch activity only, not review approval.

PPWR Digital Platform06 · Item Guidance

条目指引、填写预检与模板
Item Guidance, Pre-check & Templates

Data collection → open item
  1. 在 Evidence requirements 查看法律状态、日期、最低证据、缺口和推荐项。推荐证据与法定最低项分开显示。Review legal status/date, minimum evidence, gaps and recommendations in Evidence requirements. Recommendations are separate from legal minimums.
  2. 展开五组指引：法规/术语、所需信息与文件、步骤及正反例、样本/Word 模板/校验、来源。遵守该条目的接受格式与证据标准。Open the five guidance sections: regulation/terms, expected information/documents, steps and examples, samples/Word templates/validation, and sources. Follow item-specific accepted formats and criteria.
  3. 填写并上传证据。点击 Check my entry 可进行不保存的 dry-run，查看缺口和规则提示；据此修改，再点 Save draft 或 Submit for review。Complete fields and upload evidence. Check my entry runs a non-saving dry run for gaps/hints; correct, then select Save draft or Submit for review.
  4. 客户申请 N/A 时提供事实及法规依据；专家确认后才关闭。矩阵自动 N/A 错误时，修正项目属性/BOM。Client N/A requests need factual/legal rationale and close only after expert confirmation. Correct profile/BOM if automatic matrix N/A is wrong.
预检和动态最低证据进度是辅助检查，不能替代专家对原始证据的判断。批准后字段锁定；需修改时由专家重开。
Pre-check and minimum-evidence progress are aids, not substitutes for expert review of original evidence. Approved fields lock; an expert must reopen items for edits.

Evidence Management

PPWR Digital Platform08 · Knowledge & AI

知识库与 AI 问答
Knowledge Base & AI Assistant

  1. 在 Knowledge & AI 或条目页提问；用中文或英文输入问题，阅读回答后的 [S#] 引用。Ask in Chinese/English on Knowledge & AI or an item page; review the [S#] citations.
  2. 打开来源核对原文、页码、日期和 binding 状态。若知识库未覆盖，应停止把回答当结论并请专家补充来源。Open sources to verify text, page, date and binding status. If the corpus does not cover the issue, do not treat the answer as a conclusion; ask an expert to check sources.
  3. 全文索引可能在初次启动后后台构建；未完成时助手仍可查询精选双语知识池。专家可在 AI settings 查看索引状态并重建。The full-text index may build in the background after startup; the curated bilingual pool remains available. Experts can inspect/rebuild index in AI settings.

可选 LLM / Optional language model

专家可配置 Azure OpenAI 或兼容端点。模型仅接收检索来源，错误时回退本地模式；API key 由环境变量时以环境设置优先。密钥不应放入源码或演示材料。

Experts may configure Azure OpenAI or a compatible endpoint. The model sees retrieved sources only and errors fall back to local mode; environment configuration takes precedence. Never put keys in source code or demo materials.

受版权保护的标准仅按标题索引，不复制受保护全文。回答及来源摘要为辅助内容，须核对适用正式文本。
Copyrighted standards are indexed by title only; protected text is not reproduced. Answers/source summaries are aids; verify the applicable official text.

PPWR Digital Platform09 · Expert Knowledge Update

专家人工更新知识库
Expert Manual Knowledge Update

Knowledge & AI → Manual update · Expert only
  1. 准备官方法规原文 PDF/HTML，进入 ＋ Manual update 并上传，选择 Read the act & draft the entry。文本逐页读取；已知法规可使用法规组预置草稿，否则从文本提取，或在配置 LLM 时生成草稿。此步骤不会发布。Prepare the official act as PDF/HTML, open ＋ Manual update, upload and select Read the act & draft the entry. Text is read page by page; known acts may use regulatory-desk notes, otherwise text extraction or an optional configured LLM drafts it. Nothing is published yet.
  2. 核对法规编号、类型、约束力、中英文标题/内容、官方公报与适用日期、原文页码、影响、条款和目录项。检查来源文件是否准确。Verify reference, kind, binding status, bilingual title/text, publication/application dates, source page, impact, articles and catalogue items. Confirm the source file.
  3. 仔细审核 AI rule carried by this act。核对规则类型、条款、限值、适用关键词、制造/投放截止日期及每项条件和证据关键词。无需规则时关闭 apply in the AI pre-review。Carefully review AI rule carried by this act: type, provision, limit, scope terms, manufacturing/market deadlines and every condition/evidence keyword. Disable apply in the AI pre-review if no rule is warranted.
  4. 专家审核确认后点 Publish to the knowledge base & re-run AI。发布后创建知识条目和法规动态；相关开放条目重新预审。After expert verification, select Publish to the knowledge base & re-run AI. Publication creates a knowledge entry/update and reruns pre-review on touched open items.
AI 起草内容不是官方解释；尤其不要未经核实发布法律级别、豁免范围、期限或关键词。发布会立刻改变检索和规则判断，属于有影响的专家操作。
AI-drafted text is not an official interpretation. Never publish legal level, derogation scope, dates or keywords without verification. Publication immediately changes retrieval/rule judgement and is a consequential expert action.

How New Knowledge Changes AI Judgement

演示规则案例 / Demonstration rule case

项目 PRJ-2026-003 的水晶玻璃包装 C04-16：演示前超 Art 5(4) 限值为 fail；发布 Delegated Decision (EU) 2026/1948 的经审核规则后，范围关键词匹配时改为“可能适用豁免，需核查”，并检查制造截止日、投放截止日和条件证据。非水晶玻璃包装不受该规则影响。

For crystal-glass C04-16 in demo project PRJ-2026-003, a result above the Art 5(4) limit is initially fail. After publishing the reviewed rule for Delegated Decision (EU) 2026/1948, matching scope is flagged “derogation may apply; verify” and checked against manufacturing/market deadlines and evidence conditions. Non-crystal packaging is outside the rule.

PPWR Digital Platform11 · Suppliers

供应商、证书与变更
Suppliers, Certificates & Changes

  1. 在供应商库建立供应商和材料，记录 trace code；添加证书类型、编号、签发方、结果及期限。将 BOM 组件链接至相应供应商材料。Create suppliers/materials with trace codes; add certificate type, number, issuer, result and dates. Link BOM components to supplier materials.
  2. 仪表板提示 30 天内到期证书；续期后确认旧证书的 superseded 状态并查看 Change impact。The dashboard flags certificates expiring within 30 days. After renewal, verify superseded status and check Change impact.
  3. 报告材料/供应商变化或处置到期事件。沿材料→组件→单元→项目→条目→文件检查影响。确认后应用变更，重新提交受影响条目并更新文件。Report a material/supplier change or handle an expiry event. Trace material→components→units→projects→items→documents. Apply confirmed change, resubmit affected items and update documents.
